Cluehouse

Fog has crept up the hill, and the old house at the corner has lit its windows again, one by one, though no one has lived there in a very long time.

Tonight the house has a guest: you. Every door you pass through will lock behind you, and each room keeps one secret. Solve it and the next door creaks open. Solve all six and the house lets you out.

The ghost plays fair: everything you need is in the photos.
